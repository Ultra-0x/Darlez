How it works

Simple request steps for patients and healthcare facilities.

Diagnostic testing

  1. 1
    Choose your tests

    Browse the diagnostic catalog and add the tests you need to your request.

  2. 2
    Choose home collection

    At checkout, request home sample collection and provide your address and contact details.

  3. 3
    Confirm your visit

    Send your request on WhatsApp. The Darlez team will confirm service availability and appointment details.

    Confirm with Darlez
